Tell us

The intention of Tap Watch is to be community driven and for the people. That’s why I want to hear from you! Tell me what’s broken, what’s missing, or what you think could improve the site. The messages land on the Tap Watch admin dashboard, and there's no reply address, so leave one in the message if you'd like a response.

To add a beer or fix a tap list, it's quicker to use Submit a beer or Send photos of the taps on the pub listing itself.
