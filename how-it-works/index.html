Tap Watch , E17

How it works

Tap Watch shows which Walthamstow pubs have your beer on tap, and how recently someone checked. It covers draught beer and cider only: cask and keg, not bottles or cans.

Where the lists come from

Each pub's tap list starts from a visit or a photo of the bar. After that, it's kept up to date by people drinking there.

Still on?

At the pub, open it on Tap Watch and tap:

  • 👍 if a beer is still on. Its date updates straight away.
  • 👎 if it has gone. It's marked as reported gone. If someone else also says it's gone, it comes off the list.

Each phone can vote on each beer once a day.

What the colours mean

  • Green checked in the last 8 weeks
  • Amber checked 2 to 6 months ago
  • Grey not checked for over 6 months, so it may well have changed
  • A dashed outline someone has reported this beer but nobody else has confirmed it yet

Adding to the list

Spotted a beer that isn't listed, or a list that's out of date? Open the pub and use Submit a beer or Send photos of the taps. Everything sent in is checked before it goes on the site.

A beer someone submits goes on the pub's list marked Someone says it's on, at the top where it can be seen, until somebody else in that pub taps 👍 for it. Then it becomes an ordinary listing with today's date. You can't confirm your own report: it takes two people. Photos are different, because the picture shows the bar, so an approved photo counts as checked straight away. If nobody confirms a submitted beer within 8 weeks, it quietly comes off the list.

A photo counts from the day it was taken, not the day it was sent, so sending last week's picture won't make a pub look freshly checked.

Only regular beers are listed, not one-off guest beers.

Distances

Distances are measured in a straight line from where you are, or from a postcode you enter. Phone location can be a few hundred metres out, so a postcode is often more accurate.

Light and dark

The site turns dark from sunset to sunrise in Walthamstow. Choose Light or Dark at the bottom of the main page if you'd rather keep one.
