## Why isn't my location working?

Usually your phone's privacy settings. Phones keep a separate setting for web browsers, so
Google Maps can be spot on while the browser is given only a rough area. Here's where to
check:

- **iPhone or iPad:** Settings → Privacy & Security → Location Services → your browser
  (Safari Websites, or Chrome) → **While Using the App**, with **Precise Location** on.
- **Android:** Settings → Location → App permissions → your browser → allow while in use,
  with **Use precise location** on.

Then reload Tap Watch and tap **Use my location** again. If it's still out, enter a postcode
instead: it works just as well, and often better indoors.
