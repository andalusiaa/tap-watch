# Privacy

Tap Watch keeps as little about you as it can. There are no accounts, and it never asks for your name or email. We all want to be free from data harvesting, so this service is designed not to track you or keep tabs on you without you knowing.

## Your location

If you let Tap Watch use your location, distances are worked out on your phone. Your location is never sent to Tap Watch or stored.

If you type a postcode instead, it's sent to [postcodes.io](https://postcodes.io), a free public service, to find where it is. Tap Watch doesn't keep it.

## Votes, beer submissions and photos

Tap Watch never stores your internet (IP) address. To stop people voting many times, each vote or submission is stored with a scrambled code made from your internet address and browser type. The code can't be turned back into your address; the way it’s created changes every 30 days, and codes are wiped after 30 days.

While the code is kept, the admin dashboard of Tap Watch can report when several votes, photos or submissions came from the same device, shown as a random made-up name like "Amber Otter 17". This helps spot spam and appreciate regular helpers. It never shows who you are.

Photos of the taps:

- have hidden details such as location and phone model removed, once on your phone and again on our server

- keep one thing only: the date the picture was taken, so an older photo dates the tap list correctly rather than looking like today's news

- are only seen by the person who checks them, and are never published

- are deleted once they've been checked, or after 30 days

Please don't include people in your photos. Any note you add is kept with the report.

## Messages you send us

A message sent through [Tell us](/feedback/) is stored with the same scrambled code as everything else, so repeat messages can be spotted. Discarded messages are deleted outright; kept ones stay on a private page of ideas. Don't put anything private in a message. There's no reply address, so add your own if you want one.

## On your device

Tap Watch saves two small things in your browser, never sent anywhere:

- which beers you've voted on in the last 24 hours, so the buttons show you've voted

- your Light or Dark choice, if you've made one

There are no advertising or tracking cookies, and no analytics.

## Other services

- The site is hosted by [Cloudflare](https://www.cloudflare.com/privacypolicy/). Like any web host, Cloudflare handles your internet address to deliver pages. Tap Watch has Cloudflare's visit logging switched off.

- The map and its fonts load from Tap Watch itself, not from Google or other map companies. To keep load times fast, the map only loads the area of Walthamstow - you can’t browse further afield (not that you’d have any need to at this stage).

- "Open in maps" opens Google Maps so you can navigate to the pub, which has its own privacy policy. Nothing is sent to Google unless you tap it.
