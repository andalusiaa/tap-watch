# Questions

## Why isn't my location working?

Usually your phone's privacy settings. Phones keep a separate setting for web browsers, so Google Maps can be spot on while the browser is given only a rough area. Here's where to check:

- **iPhone or iPad:** Settings → Privacy & Security → Location Services → your browser (Safari Websites, or Chrome) → **While Using the App**, with **Precise Location** on.

- **Android:** Settings → Location → App permissions → your browser → allow while in use, with **Use precise location** on.

Then reload Tap Watch and tap **Use my location** again. If it's still out, enter a postcode instead: it works just as well, and often better indoors.

## Can I keep Tap Watch on my home screen?

Yes, and it's the quickest way in. It opens like an app, with no address bar, but doesn’t require you to download anything.

- **iPhone or iPad:** open Tap Watch in Safari, tap the share button at the bottom, then **Add to Home Screen**.

- **Android:** open Tap Watch in Chrome, tap the three dots, then **Add to Home screen**.

## How up to date are the lists?

Each beer shows when someone last checked it: green within 8 weeks, amber up to 6 months, grey after that. Pubs change their taps often, so treat an old date as a maybe. A 👍 from you at the bar updates it on the spot.

## A beer is missing, or the list is wrong

Open the pub and use **Submit a beer**, or **Send photos of the taps**. Everything is checked by a person before it appears. Photos count from the day they were taken, so an older photo won't make a pub look freshly checked.

## Why are some pubs empty?

Because nobody has been in yet. Tap Watch is new and the lists are filled in pub by pub.

## Do you track me?

No. There are no accounts, no advertising cookies and no analytics. Your location stays on your phone. See [privacy](/privacy/) for the detail.
